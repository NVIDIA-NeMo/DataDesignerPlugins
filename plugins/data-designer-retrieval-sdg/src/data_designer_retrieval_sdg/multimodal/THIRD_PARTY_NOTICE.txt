The query templates, module definitions, heading/TOC section planning, summary
grading and selection/clustering algorithms are adapted from illuin-tech/vidore-v3-generation at revision
2cfd2f78b8ae4f4bedd87d67f373dc51c12a856e (https://github.com/illuin-tech/vidore-v3-generation).
They operate on the generic retrieval source contract; no benchmark dataset
loader, filenames, or external source checkout is required. Optional generic
page_number metadata is used only for corroborated TOC alignment; source units
are never split or reconstructed from parsed markdown offsets.
